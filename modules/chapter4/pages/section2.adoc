= Operator Deployment LAB

This section uses the *Product Enablement AAP Operator Deployment* lab. Please be aware that setting up an operator lab will take about 50 minutes. Once it is ready, proceed with the following installation steps:

The following video walks through installing the Ansible Automation Platform 2.6 Operator:

video::aap2.6_operator_install.mp4[align="left",width=800,height=500]


All steps shown in the video are listed below:

. Log in to the Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform.
. Navigate to **Operators → OperatorHub**.
. Search for *Ansible Automation Platform* and click *Install*.
. Select the **stable-2.6** channel.
. Select the default namespace (**aap**).
. Click *Install*.
. Go to *Installed Operators*. Once the *Ansible Automation Platform* operator appears, click on it.

. If not already selected, set the project to **aap**.

. Scroll to *Ansible Automation Platform* and click **Create instance** to deploy all AAP components.

. Set **Name** to `aap`. This name is used when creating associated resources such as network routes and secrets.
+
NOTE: Many customization options are available, but this lab covers a basic deployment only.

. Click **Create**. The resources will deploy in approximately 20 minutes.

. On the OpenShift dashboard, navigate to **Networking → Routes** and click the route named *ansible* (or the name you specified in Step 10).

. On the OpenShift dashboard, navigate to **Workloads → Secrets** and search for *admin*. Click the secret and copy the *password*.

. Attach a Red Hat subscription to activate AAP. You can use a 60-day trial subscription, a *Red Hat Developer Subscription for Individuals*, or upload a *Manifest file*.

. Submit the subscription and run a *Project sync* on the *Demo Project* to verify that jobs execute successfully.
